Features

Everything about your home, organized

A warm, private home for the things that matter — fast to fill in and easy to trust.

Quick answer

Dewllio is a private home-inventory app that keeps every belonging, warranty, receipt, and home-maintenance task in one place. Catalog items with photos and receipts, get reminded before warranties lapse, schedule recurring upkeep, organize everything by room with instant search, and export it all to CSV — private, secure, and ad-free.

One connected home

Built to work as a whole

Dewllio isn't six separate tools bolted together — it's one calm place for your home. The moment you add an item with its receipt, its warranty and any recurring upkeep can hang off the same record. Rooms and search keep growing lists navigable, and CSV export means nothing is ever locked in.
