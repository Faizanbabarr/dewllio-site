Maintenance

Home upkeep that runs itself

Filters, smoke alarms, gutters, HVAC — set a cadence once and let Dewllio remember.

Quick answer

Create a maintenance task — like replacing an HVAC filter, testing smoke alarms, or cleaning gutters — choose how often it should repeat, and Dewllio reminds you each time it's due. Mark tasks complete to keep a tidy history of your home's upkeep.

Set it once

Set a repeating cadence

Every home has a rhythm of small jobs, and it's easy to lose track of which one is next. In Dewllio you add a maintenance task, describe what needs doing, and choose how often it should repeat — monthly, quarterly, twice a year, once a year, or whatever interval fits the job. That cadence is saved with the task, so you decide the schedule a single time instead of re-thinking it every season.

Because each task carries its own cadence, a monthly furnace-filter swap and a yearly dryer-vent cleaning can happily live side by side. When you mark the current one done, Dewllio rolls the schedule forward to the next occurrence — no spreadsheets, no sticky notes, no mental math about "was that in the spring or the fall?"

Never forget

Reminders when tasks are due

A schedule only helps if something nudges you at the right moment. Dewllio sends a notification as a task comes due, so upkeep surfaces on its own instead of waiting for you to remember it. The filter gets changed before airflow drops; the smoke alarms get tested before the batteries quietly die.

It's the same gentle, on-device reminder engine that powers Dewllio's warranty reminders — so the day a coverage window is about to close and the day the gutters are due can both land as a simple, timely heads-up. One place, one habit, fewer things falling through the cracks.

Proof of care

A tidy history of completed upkeep

When a task is done, you mark it complete — and that single tap does double duty. It moves the task forward to its next due date, and it leaves a record of when the work happened. Over time those records add up to a clear picture of how your home has been looked after.

That history is genuinely useful: it answers "when did I last flush the water heater?" at a glance, shows a buyer or landlord that a place has been maintained, and pairs naturally with your home inventory so the appliance and the upkeep it needs live in the same app. Know what you own, and know you've kept it running well.

Ideas to start

Common tasks to track

HVAC & furnace filters

Swap or clean filters on a monthly or quarterly cadence to keep airflow strong.

Smoke & CO alarms

Test alarms and change batteries on a set schedule so they're always ready.

Gutters & downspouts

Clear leaves and debris twice a year so water flows away from the house.

Water heater & softener

Flush the tank and top up salt on a yearly rhythm to extend their life.

Fridge & dryer

Clean coils and clear the dryer vent to run efficiently and safely.

Seasonal home checks

Reverse ceiling fans, service the mower, or seal decks when the season turns.

Tip: start with two or three tasks you already forget, set their cadence, and add more as the seasons come around. Small, steady upkeep beats one big catch-up day.
