Inventory

A home inventory you'll actually keep

Capture everything you own in seconds — with the photo, receipt, and details that matter.

Quick answer

Open Dewllio, tap +, and add an item in about 30 seconds: name it, snap a photo, pick a room, and attach the receipt plus details like purchase date, price, brand, and serial number. Every item is searchable instantly and backed up to your private account.

Fast capture

Add an item in seconds

Most inventory apps ask for a form so long you give up after five things. Dewllio flips that: tap the + button, give the item a name, and you already have a real entry. A quick photo and a room, and you're done — usually in under half a minute.

Everything else is optional and can wait. You can breeze through your whole living room first, then circle back later to add prices, serial numbers, or receipts when you have a spare minute. The point is momentum: an inventory only protects you if you actually finish it.

One place

Photos & receipts, kept together

Right now the photo of your TV lives in your camera roll, its receipt is buried in an email, and the warranty card is in a drawer somewhere. Dewllio keeps all three in one place — attach a photo of the product and its receipt or document to the same item, so proof of purchase never drifts away from the thing it belongs to.

Because receipts are captured as you go, the paperwork is already there when you need it — for a return, a repair, or a warranty reminder down the line. Photos and documents live in your private, backed-up account, not scattered across apps.

Be claim-ready

The details that matter for insurance & claims

If something is ever lost, stolen, or damaged, the difference between a smooth claim and a frustrating one is documentation. Dewllio gives every item a field for the things an insurer or adjuster tends to ask about:

$

Purchase price & date

Record what you paid and when, so replacement value and depreciation are easy to establish.

#

Serial number & brand

Note the serial, brand, and model — the identifiers that prove the specific item was yours.

✓

Photo & receipt

Keep a clear photo and the original receipt attached as evidence of ownership and condition.

Tip: add these details for your highest-value belongings first — electronics, appliances, jewelry, and tools. A short session now saves a stressful scramble later.
Findable

Always organized and searchable

Adding things is only half the job — you also need to find them again. In Dewllio, every item is searchable the moment you save it. Look something up by name, brand, or where it lives, and it surfaces instantly.

Items are grouped by room, so you can browse the garage or the kitchen the way you'd actually walk through your home. Explore how rooms & search keep a growing inventory tidy, and when you need a copy outside the app you can pull the whole list as a spreadsheet. Your inventory stays yours, private and backed up, from the first item to the thousandth.
