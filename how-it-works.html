How it works

Three steps to total peace of mind

Set up your home once, capture your things, and let Dewllio handle the reminders.

Quick answer

Set up your home once (about a minute), capture your belongings with photos, warranties, and receipts, then relax — Dewllio reminds you before warranties lapse and maintenance is due. It's private, ad-free, and works on iOS and Android.

3 steps

From empty to organized

1

Add your home

Create your home and set up rooms — kitchen, garage, living room, wherever your things live. It takes about a minute, and you can add or rename rooms any time. This is the frame everything else hangs on, so your inventory stays organized by where things actually are.

2

Capture your things

Snap a photo of an item, add the details that matter — brand, model, serial number, purchase date — and attach the warranty and receipt. Do it as you go; there's no bulk import to finish before the app pays off. Every item lives in a room, so you always know what you own and where it is.

3

Relax

Once a warranty expiry or a recurring maintenance task is in place, Dewllio does the remembering. It reminds you before warranties lapse and before maintenance is due — so you claim while coverage is still valid and stay ahead of the filter changes and service dates that are easy to forget.

Under the hood

What you can track

Those three steps cover the whole loop, but each one opens up into more. Dewllio is built around a handful of connected pieces — your inventory, the warranties and receipts attached to it, the maintenance reminders that keep your home running, and the rooms that hold it all together. Here's what each part does.

Home inventory

A searchable record of everything you own, with photos and the details you'd need for a claim, a resale, or a move.

Warranty tracking

Log each warranty's expiry and get reminded before it lapses, so you fix or replace on the manufacturer's dime instead of yours.

Maintenance reminders

Set recurring home tasks — HVAC filters, smoke-alarm batteries, gutter cleaning — and let Dewllio nudge you before each is due.

Rooms & organization

Group items by the room they live in, so everything you own has a place and you can find it fast when it matters.

Privacy & security

Your inventory is yours alone — private storage, no ads, and no selling of your data. Peace of mind is the point.

Export your data

Take your records with you whenever you want — useful for an insurance claim, an estate, or simply your own backup.
