Account & data

Delete your account

Permanently remove your Dewllio account and all associated data.

You can permanently delete your Dewllio account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app:

1

Open Dewllio and sign in.

2

Go to Settings → Delete account.

3

Type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. We permanently remove your account and all of the following: homes, inventory items, item photos, receipts and warranty documents, maintenance tasks and completion history, reminder schedules, device records, and your authentication account. Subscription records held by Apple or Google are governed by their respective stores.

Prefer we do it for you?

If you can't access the app, email support@dewllio.com from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We will verify ownership and delete your account and data within 30 days.

Data retention: we do not retain personal data after deletion, except where required by law.
