How to join

1

Request access

Email support@dewllio.com and tell us the Google account email on your Android phone. We'll add you to the tester list (usually the same day).

2

Opt in

On your phone (signed into that same Google account), tap Become a tester above and accept. Give it a minute to register.

3

Install & explore

Open Dewllio on Google Play and install. Add a few items, set a warranty reminder, and tell us what you think — you're in! 🎉

Note: the beta is invite-only, so the button only works once your email is on the tester list. iOS testing (TestFlight) is coming soon — email us to be first in line.
